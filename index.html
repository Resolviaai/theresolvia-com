Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before partnering with our editing studio.

01

What is Resolvia?

Resolvia is an elite video editing studio and agency. We partner with leading creators, founders, and personal brands to edit viral, retention-engineered videos that build massive audiences and scale revenue.

02

What tools do you edit with?

Our editors work exclusively in Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, and DaVinci Resolve Studio. Every video receives custom sound effects, professional typography, calibrated color passes, and 4K masters.

03

Which platforms do you cover?

We produce vertical short-form for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts, as well as full-length YouTube videos, documentary edits, and high-converting paid ad creatives.

04

What is your turnaround time?

Standard delivery is 24 to 48 hours per video cut. We work in dedicated Slack or WhatsApp channels directly with you and your team for instant feedback and iteration.

05

Do you offer revisions?

Yes! We provide unlimited revisions on all edits until you are thrilled with the cut. We lock in your style guide and creator profile so every edit hits the mark immediately.

06

How do we get started?

Simply click "Book a Call". We review your current content, define your hook and editing style, and edit your first sample video within 48 hours.
