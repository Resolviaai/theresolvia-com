## 1. Video Editing Pricing Models Explained

Before commissioning an edit, it is critical to understand how editors and post-production studios structure their fees:

| Pricing Model | Typical Cost Range | Best Suited For | Key Trade-off |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hourly Rate** | $35 – $120 / hour | Irregular or experimental edits | Unpredictable final invoice |
| **Per-Video Fixed** | $350 – $1,800 / edit | Single milestone launches | Revision caps often strictly enforced |
| **Monthly Retainer** | $2,500 – $7,500 / mo | Consistent creators (4-8 edits/mo) | Guaranteed SLA, dedicated lead editor |

## 2. What Actually Drives Post-Production Costs?

Two 10-minute videos can require wildly different amounts of labor. A static talking head might take 4 hours to complete, whereas an investigative video essay can demand 30 to 40 hours of intensive timeline surgery. The 5 core cost drivers are:

1. **Shooting Ratio & Raw Ingestion:** Sifting through 6 hours of multi-cam footage takes significantly more time than a single-take script.
2. **Retention Pacing & Foley Design:** High-retention edits require sound effects (foley) synchronized to every motion element, risers for narrative tension, and music frequency carving.
3. **Motion Graphics & 3D Animation:** Custom kinetic text, 3D title cards, and animated screen mockups require After Effects and Cinema 4D mastery.
4. **Color Grading & Mastering:** Professional ACES/DaVinci Resolve color matching across disparate camera bodies (Sony, RED, iPhone).
5. **Turnaround Speed:** A 24-48 hour sprint delivery carries a premium over a 7-day turnaround.
