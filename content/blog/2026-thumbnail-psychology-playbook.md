## The 3 Visual Rules for 2026 Packaging

### Rule 1: The 1-Second Focal Test
Squint at your thumbnail at mobile scale (120px wide). If the primary subject and emotional tension cannot be recognized within 1 second, the visual composition is too cluttered.

### Rule 2: Don't Repeat the Title
If your video title is "Why Most Startups Fail in 2026", never put "Startups Fail" in the thumbnail text. The thumbnail should show the symptom or emotion: "The $10M Mistake".

### Rule 3: Immediate Video Payoff
The thumbnail promise must be visually validated in the first 3 seconds of the video edit to prevent viewer bounce.
