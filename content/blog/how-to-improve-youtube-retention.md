## 1. The 4-Beat Retention Architecture

YouTube's recommendation algorithm prioritizes satisfaction signals, primarily Average View Duration (AVD) and Relative Retention. Here is the exact structural editing system Resolvia implements for creator channels:

### Beat 1: The 0–3 Second Visual Interrupt
Confirm the thumbnail promise immediately without repeating the title verbatim. Use movement, face tracking, and a punchy sonic impact to prevent the initial bounce.

### Beat 2: Premise Confirmation (0:03 – 0:45)
Set the stakes and introduce the "curiosity gap." The viewer must understand what problem is being solved and why leaving now means missing the revelation.

### Beat 3: Cognitive Escalation (Every 30 Seconds)
Human focus deteriorates after 25 seconds of visual uniformity. We insert camera angle crops, kinetic overlays, or music shift cues every 20-30 seconds to refresh attention.

### Beat 4: The Frictionless Outro Bridge
Never say "in conclusion" or "thank you for watching": viewers leave instantly. Deliver the final insight and immediately bridge to the next recommended video card.
