## 1. Why In-House Video Teams Create Fragile Margins for Agencies

Hiring full-time video editors requires salary, healthcare, workstation hardware ($5,000+ per editing rig), DaVinci and Adobe licenses, and asset subscriptions (music, foley, stock). When client retainers fluctuate, agencies are left with heavy fixed overhead. A white-label partner provides elastic, on-demand capacity that scales seamlessly up or down.

## 2. The 3 Non-Negotiable Requirements of an Agency Partnership

1. **Strict Bilateral NDAs:** The post-production team must never solicit, contact, or publicly display client work without explicit written authorization.
2. **Guaranteed Turnaround SLA:** A rigid 48-to-72-hour first cut turnaround ensures the agency never misses client publishing deadlines.
3. **Frame.io White-Label Review:** Review links can be shared directly with your client without any third-party agency branding.
