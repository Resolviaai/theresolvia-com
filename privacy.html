Privacy & Media Protection Protocol

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: November 2026 · Applies to Resolvia Creative Studio
Creator Assurance Summary: Resolvia Creative Studio provides post-production and creative direction services to video creators and brands. We treat all unreleased video rushes, audio stems, project files, and scripts as strictly confidential trade secrets under bilateral NDA protection. We never train generative AI models on your private raw media.

01. Scope & Studio Commitment

This Privacy Policy applies to all creator partnerships, retainer accounts, and prospective clients interacting with Resolvia Creative Studio ("Resolvia", "we", "us", or "our") via theresolvia.com, our Frame.io portal, private Discord/Slack production channels, and direct cloud transmission.

We are a dedicated post-production studio, not a public file-sharing service. Your footage and identity are never commoditized or exposed to unauthorized parties.

02. Media Data We Collect & Process

In order to provide retention-engineered post-production services, we collect and process:

  • Raw Media Assets: Uncut camera rushes, screen recordings, B-roll, A-roll video files, high-fidelity audio WAV stems, voiceovers, and LUT profiles provided via Google Drive, Dropbox, MASV, or Frame.io.
  • Creative Direction Materials: Video scripts, outlines, retention cues, timecode markers, brand style guides, fonts, color palettes, and motion design references.
  • Commercial & Onboarding Data: Creator legal name, brand handle, email address, timezone, Discord/Slack handle, and invoicing information.
  • Review Feedback: Time-stamped comments, revision requests, and audio notes submitted during rough cut reviews.

03. Pre-Release Confidentiality & Leak Prevention

For high-growth creators and digital media personalities, unreleased content represents significant commercial value. A pre-mature leak can compromise sponsorship deals, YouTube algorithm launches, and exclusive announcements.

Our Zero-Leak Protocol: All editors assigned to your account operate under legally binding bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). Raw media files are housed on access-controlled local SSD arrays and private team clouds protected with two-factor authentication (2FA).

Media assets are strictly accessible only by the primary lead editor, motion designer, sound engineer, and creative director assigned to your specific project sprint.

04. Generative AI & Model Training Restrictions

Resolvia operates with strict respect for creator intellectual property:

  • We NEVER license, ingest, or feed your raw rushes, voice recordings, or finished videos into external generative AI training models.
  • Any proprietary AI tools utilized by our studio (e.g. noise reduction, speech isolation, rotoscoping, transcription) operate strictly within closed, non-retaining enterprise APIs where data is immediately discarded post-processing.

05. Storage Duration & Project Archival

We maintain an organized cold-storage pipeline to safeguard your work in case emergency revisions or asset re-exports are needed:

  • Active Production: Raw assets and project files (Premiere Pro .prproj, DaVinci .drp, After Effects .aep) reside on active RAID arrays for the duration of the editing sprint.
  • Post-Delivery Grace Period: Final exported masters and project archives are retained for sixty (60) days following final approval to facilitate localized adjustments.
  • Purge on Demand: Creators may request immediate permanent deletion of all raw rushes and working files at any time by emailing studio@theresolvia.com.

06. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner only with industry-standard, SOC-2 compliant infrastructure providers for secure pipeline management:

  • Frame.io (Adobe): Encrypted video review and collaborative timecode feedback.
  • Stripe: PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant retainer billing and invoice settlement (Resolvia never stores credit card details).
  • Google Workspace / AWS S3: Enterprise cloud storage with AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit.

07. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Regardless of your geographic location, you retain full sovereignty over your creative assets and personal information:

  • Right to Erase: Request immediate destruction of raw assets and personal records.
  • Right to Export: Request transfer of completed project archives, LUTs, and motion project files.
  • Right to Rectify: Update billing, producer contact, or creator channel records at any time.
