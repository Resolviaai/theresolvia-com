## Long-form YouTube video editing

URL: https://theresolvia.com/services/youtube-video-editing/

End-to-end post-production for long-form YouTube videos, structured around narrative pacing and audience retention.

Includes:

- Structural narrative pacing and retention-curve optimization
- Sonic foley design and custom sound design
- Fairlight audio mastering, with dialogue mastered to the broadcast loudness standard of -14 LUFS
- Color grading in DaVinci Resolve Studio
- 3D motion graphics and kinetic titles (2D/3D work in Adobe After Effects)
- High-CTR thumbnail packaging, with 2-3 thumbnail variations per edit
- A 4-beat retention framework: sub-3-second hook interrupt, micro-tension staging every 20-30 seconds, sonic foley resets, and transition bridges

## Short-form video editing

URL: https://theresolvia.com/services/short-form-video-editing/

High-velocity vertical video editing (9:16) for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts, built for the sub-3-second scroll test.

Includes:

- Sub-3-second hooks and platform-native retention pacing
- Kinetic typography and kinetic captions
- Dynamic sound FX and audio soundscapes
- Rhythmic high-speed cutting tuned for completion rate
- Reformatting of long-form source footage into 15 to 90-second vertical assets
- Extraction of 10-15 vertical clips from a single 45-60 minute raw video or podcast episode

## Podcast video editing

URL: https://theresolvia.com/services/podcast-video-editing/

Post-production for video podcasts, covering multi-camera feeds, broadcast loudness mastering, and vertical clip repurposing.

- Multi-camera video synchronization and dynamic switching between hosts and guests
- Dialogue cleaning and Fairlight broadcast audio mastering to -14 LUFS
- Color grading
- 10-15 viral vertical clips per episode, with kinetic subtitles, sound FX, and intro hooks

## Video ad editing
