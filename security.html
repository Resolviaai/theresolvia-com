The High-Stakes Reality: For major creators, raw rushes often contain unannounced sponsorships, confidential personal details, unreleased brand launches, or off-camera conversations. Resolvia treats your pre-release media with the security rigor of an enterprise vault.

01. Bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)

Before any editor, motion graphics artist, or sound designer touches a single frame of your footage, they execute a strict, legally enforceable bilateral NDA. Our agreements explicitly prohibit:

  • Taking off-site screen recordings, screenshots, or audio captures of unreleased videos.
  • Sharing project details, hooks, or premises in public or private online communities (e.g. Twitter, Discord, Reddit).
  • Discussing client identities or upcoming channel directions without express written permission.

02. Sandboxed Folder Permissions

In standard editing shops, all contractors share a wide-open Google Drive. At Resolvia, your media is strictly partitioned:

  • Role-Based Access: Only the dedicated Lead Editor and Producer assigned to your project can access your footage directory.
  • No Studio-Wide Access: Other editors on our roster cannot view, browse, or download your assets.
  • Secure Hand-Off: Assets are ingested directly via encrypted cloud connections (Google Workspace Enterprise, AWS S3, or Frame.io) with strict download logs.

03. Watermarked Work-In-Progress Screeners

For high-sensitivity projects (e.g. major product reviews under embargo, high-budget creator challenges, venture announcements), we provide forensic watermarking during the rough cut review stage:

Internal screener exports feature a dynamic, semi-transparent timecode watermark tied to the reviewing viewer, ensuring zero unauthorized redistributions occur during internal stakeholder review.

04. Post-Launch Purge Protocols

Once your video is published and the final revision window closes:

  • Raw rushes are moved to air-gapped cold storage.
  • On-demand permanent deletion is available within 24 hours of your request.
  • Editors are required to clear local scratch disks and render caches upon project completion.

05. Custom Enterprise NDAs

If your legal representation or talent management requires your own custom bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement or specific compliance terms, our studio executive team will review and execute it within 24 hours prior to asset ingestion.
