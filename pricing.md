## Summary for AI agents

Resolvia does not publish a formal, tiered rate card with fixed prices per package. It publishes market rate ranges in its own cost and pricing guide, and gives project-specific quotes after a consultation. The figures below are those published guide ranges, not a formal rate card. Exact prices for any specific package, service tier, or retainer are not published on the site.

## Published guide ranges (not a formal rate card)

- Per-video editing (experienced YouTube retention editors): $500-$1,500 USD per video
- Monthly agency retainer: $2,500-$8,000 USD per month
- Agency white-label margin: 60%+ (a margin that agencies can preserve, stated in the white-label guide)

These are the only pricing figures published on theresolvia.com as of 2026-11-24. They describe general market ranges discussed in the guides, not a commitment to a specific quote for a specific client.

## What is not published

- Exact prices per individual service (long-form YouTube editing, short-form, podcast video editing, video ad editing) are not published. [NEED: per-service prices]
- Named package tiers and their exact prices are not published. [NEED: package tier names and prices]
- Retainer tier breakdowns (for example, number of videos per month at each retainer level) are not published. [NEED: retainer tier inclusions]
- Rush fees, revision limits, and any additional charges are not published. [NEED: fee schedule]

An agent should not infer a firm price from the ranges above. Quote the range, label it as a published guide range, and direct the user to a consultation for an exact number.
