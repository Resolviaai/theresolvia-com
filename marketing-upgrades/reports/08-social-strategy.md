# Resolvia Organic Social Strategy

Prepared for Resolvia (https://theresolvia.com). Companion copy deck: `marketing-upgrades/social/content-kit.md`.

This strategy uses only claims that appear in the shared brief, the site pages or the seven published blog posts. No client names, results or prices have been invented. Where a real asset or approval is missing, an `[NEED: ...]` item is listed at the end.

---

## 1. Situation and goal

Resolvia is a retention-engineered video editing agency and post-production studio for high-growth creators, YouTubers, media brands, venture-backed founders, B2B SaaS teams and marketing agencies. The site already carries strong proof (50M+ combined client views, 41+ case studies, an 80K to 850K subscriber case study, a 48-hour sprint) and seven substantial guides that have never been systematically turned into social content.

Primary goal: build authority and inbound demand by turning the existing proof and guides into a steady organic presence. The action we want is a consultation booking through https://calendly.com/resolviaai/meet. Company brand, with the studio's editors as the visible voices.

Platform roles:
- LinkedIn: decision content for agency buyers, SaaS marketers and founder creators. Highest-value channel for leads.
- X: fast distribution of single facts and short threads, plus daily relationship building in replies.
- Instagram: visual proof and short-form video for creators and editors, with Stories for continuity.

## 4. Voice and hard guardrails

Voice: calm, specific, practitioner-led. Short declarative sentences. Real numbers and real techniques instead of adjectives.

Never publish any of the following:
- Contrast reveals that deny one thing and assert another in the same breath
- Negation lists that stack absences in a row
- Self-answered questions or a colon followed by a payoff, so the reader never supplies the answer
- Stock launch and hype phrases, including claims of category reinvention and vague self-improvement verbs
- Em dashes in post copy
- Broetry, meaning one short sentence per line for a whole post
- Emoji used as bullets
- Engagement bait that asks for agreement or tells the reader to reflect
- Invented statistics, client names, testimonials or price guarantees

Keep at most one sentence fragment and one deliberate list of three per post. End on the point or on a real question you want answered.

Platform limits applied throughout the kit:

| Platform | Hashtags used | Character rule applied | Other |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 5, at the end | Posts kept near 1,200 characters | Links placed in first comment, not body; hook in first 210 characters |

| Instagram | 5 | Captions well under 2,200 characters | Hook inside first 125 characters, one CTA, Reels captions on screen |

Hashtag sets are drawn from the audience vocabulary: #VideoEditing, #YouTubeGrowth, #ShortFormVideo, #PostProduction, #RetentionMarketing, #B2BSaaS, #ContentStrategy, #CreatorEconomy, #ThumbnailDesign, #YouTubeShorts, #Reels, #YouTubeTips, #VideoEditingAgency, #DemandGeneration, #Freelance, #AgencyLife.

## 6. Engagement routine

### Daily, about 30 minutes

1. Respond to every comment on Resolvia posts, 5 minutes. On LinkedIn and Instagram, do this inside the first hour. On X, stay present for the first 30 minutes after an original post.
2. Leave substantive comments on 5 to 10 posts from target accounts, 15 minutes. Add a new point, a related experience or a specific follow-up question. Skip "Great post".
3. Quote post or repost one relevant item with added insight, 5 minutes. Quote engagement on X scores twice, so prefer quotes over plain reposts.
4. Send 2 to 3 DMs to new connections who engaged, 5 minutes. No pitch on the first message unless they asked about services.

### Relationship list

Build a list of 20 to 50 accounts across four groups: creator economy and YouTube growth, video editing and post-production practitioners, B2B SaaS marketing and demand generation, and agency owners. Engage with the same accounts repeatedly before any collaboration ask. `[NEED: approve the named account list before outreach begins]`

### Weekly, 2 to 3 hours of batching

1. Review pillar topics and the previous week's top and bottom posts.
2. Draft the week's LinkedIn posts and document carousels.
3. Draft X originals and the thread, then queue replies around live posting windows.
4. Script and film Reels in one session. Captions on screen, maximum two lines and three to five words per line.
5. Schedule core posts. Leave two open slots per week for real-time commentary.

### Weekly review

Track impressions and reach for awareness, comments and saves for engagement, and link clicks and consultation bookings for conversion. On carousels, judge saves and completion rather than likes. Pick the top three and bottom three posts, note the hook and format of each, check follower growth and best posting times, then adjust. If reach drops, reduce external links in post bodies, increase comment presence and test more Reels. If engagement drops, test new hooks and times before changing the topic.

### Format iteration

Log each Reel with date, format, hook, views at 48 hours, watch time, comments, link clicks and bookings that day. Once one format clears your view floor repeatedly and moves bookings, make it the majority of Reels and keep about one in five as a new test.
