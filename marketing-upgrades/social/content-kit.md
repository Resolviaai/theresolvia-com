Most creator videos lose their audience in the first 30 seconds. The edit can change that.

Resolvia's 4-Beat System is built around the points where attention actually drops. This is the structure behind edits that reach 50% plus average view duration.

Beat 1 is the 0 to 3 second visual interrupt. Confirm the thumbnail promise right away with movement and a sonic hit. Repeating the title word for word wastes your strongest second.

Beat 2 is premise confirmation from 0:03 to 0:45. Set the stakes and open a curiosity gap the viewer wants closed.

Beat 3 is cognitive escalation every 30 seconds. Focus fades after about 25 seconds of visual uniformity, so change the camera angle, add a kinetic overlay or shift the music.

Beat 4 is the frictionless outro bridge. Deliver the final insight and move straight into the next video card.
